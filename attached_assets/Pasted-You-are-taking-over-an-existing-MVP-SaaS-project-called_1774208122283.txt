======================================
PROJECT CONTEXT
======================================

This is a focused AI-powered generator for a specific outcome:
launching a fitness challenge funnel fast.

The app helps a coach generate a complete 30-day challenge funnel by answering a short onboarding wizard.

The generated output includes:
1. Landing page copy
2. Offer structure
3. Opt-in form suggestions
4. Thank-you page copy
5. Booking page copy
6. SMS follow-up sequence
7. Email follow-up sequence
8. No-show / reminder follow-up
9. Facebook ad copy
10. Image / creative prompts
11. Basic campaign naming / UTM suggestions

The desired user feeling is:
“Wow, this gave me a launch-ready challenge funnel in minutes.”

This is not a general funnel builder.
It should feel like:
- part strategist
- part funnel copywriter
- part campaign assistant

It should not feel like:
- a generic AI writer
- a generic CRM
- a bloated funnel builder
