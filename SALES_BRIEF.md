1. **Paralysis by preparation** — "You've been 'getting ready to launch' for months." Writing copy from scratch takes 40–60 hours. Most coaches quit before they publish.

5. **A launch that flopped** — "You launched a challenge once and it flopped." The challenge content wasn't the problem — the copy, sequence, and sales process were.

**Root cause framing (important for the copy):**
These aren't personal failures. Coaches were never trained to be marketers, copywriters, or sales strategists. They were trained to coach. FitPro Launch gives them everything else.

## 4. THE SOLUTION — WHAT IT GENERATES

FitPro Launch produces 5 complete systems in one wizard run:

### System 1 — The Funnel Kit (4 assets)
Everything needed to convert a cold visitor into a booked discovery call:
- **Landing page** — Headline, benefit bullets, FAQ, urgency section, and CTA written for the coach's specific challenge
- **Opt-in form** — Recommended fields and intro copy that pre-frames value before the click
- **Thank you page** — Post-signup confirmation copy that reduces drop-off and primes for booking
- **Booking page** — Discovery call framing that makes booking feel like the obvious next step

### System 2 — The Sales Machine (4 assets)
Everything needed to convert a lead into a paying client:
- **Discovery call script** — 8-phase word-for-word script: rapport → pain discovery → future pacing → offer presentation → price reveal → close → objection handling. Tested to take close rates from 20% to 80%.
- **Pricing & offer guide** — Recommended price point, value stack framing, and confidence scripts for the money conversation
- **Instagram DM scripts** — 4 complete conversation paths: cold outreach, warm lead, comment-to-DM, inbound enquiry. Opener → follow-ups → booking bridge for each
- **Upsell email sequence** — 5-email post-challenge sequence converting completers into the coach's next-tier offer

### System 3 — The Ad Engine (4 assets)
Everything needed to run paid traffic:
- **Facebook & Instagram ad copy** — Scroll-stopping hooks, primary text, headlines, and descriptions ready to paste into Ads Manager
- **Creative briefs** — Visual concepts for 4 ad formats: static image, talking-head reel, before/after transformation, UGC-style
- **Campaign naming conventions** — UTM-structured campaign, ad set, and ad naming for clean attribution
- **VSL script** (application funnels) — 11-section video sales letter script: hook → authority → pain → mechanism → proof → offer → guarantee → close

### System 4 — The Delivery System (4 assets)
Everything needed to deliver the challenge and collect testimonials:
- **Challenge delivery pack** — Welcome email, 30 daily SMS motivational prompts, 4 weekly coaching emails, and a completion sequence
- **Pre/during/post email + SMS sequences** — Full nurture and re-engagement sequences across the challenge lifecycle
- **Testimonial harvest sequence** — 5-message automated sequence timed to collect reviews at peak client satisfaction (day 28 of a 30-day challenge)
- **52-week nurture sequence** — Year-long weekly follow-up email sequence that keeps warm leads engaged between launches, organised by quarter with themed arcs

### System 5 — The Growth Engine (4 assets)
Everything needed to stay consistent and scale:
- **30-day content calendar** — Strategy-led month of Instagram content — hooks, captions, CTAs, all written for the coach's niche and audience
- **Monthly Content Engine** — Ongoing feature: fresh 30-post plan every month, seasonally themed, with DM conversation starters and story ideas
- **Launch roadmap** — 6-phase week-by-week plan tying every FitPro Launch asset to a specific task and deadline. Phases: Setup → Content → Outreach → Ads → Launch → Scale
- **Workout plan generator** — A complete 4-week training programme for challenge participants, personalised to the challenge type

### Bonus: Application Funnel Mode
A second funnel type (in addition to challenge funnels) for high-ticket application programmes:
- 22-section registration page with full direct-response copy
- Coach story section, benefit blocks, FAQ, client stories with before/after headlines, qualification criteria, guarantee section
- VSL script for video-led application pages
- ManyChat DM flow for organic Instagram lead nurture

### Integration: 1-Click HighLevel Push
- Connect a GHL API key + location ID in Account Settings
- One click pushes all funnel pages, automations, and email/SMS sequences directly into HighLevel
- Includes a Chrome Extension for copy/paste injection into the GHL page builder
- No reformatting required

### Also Included:

## 9. BRAND VOICE & TONE

**Voice:** Direct, confident, no fluff. Not corporate. Talks to the coach like a sharp business mentor who has seen the patterns and knows exactly what's holding them back.

**Tone spectrum:** Empathetic about the struggle → brutally honest about the cause → matter-of-fact about the solution → urgent about acting now

**What to avoid:**
- Generic motivational language ("You can do it!")
- Passive hedging ("This might help you...")
- Overpromising individual income results (say "first client" not "six figures")
- Long academic sentences — punchy paragraphs, short sentences preferred

**What to include:**
- Specific numbers wherever possible (22 assets, 8-phase script, 18 clients in 4 days, 80% close rate, $2,800 saved)
- The word "today" and "this week" — immediacy is core to the brand
- Direct contrast between the old way (expensive, slow, DIY, broken) and the new way (FitPro Launch)
- The phrase "online fitness coaches" not "personal trainers" (broader appeal)
